Detailed information on the subject of data protection is set out below. Part of the information may be collected because you choose to contact Mawonga Training, while other information may be generated automatically through normal technical website use.

How do we collect your data?

Some data are collected when you provide them to us directly. This may include information you provide when you email us, call us, WhatsApp us, request information about training, or otherwise communicate with Mawonga Training.

What do we use your data for?

  • To meet legal, professional, or operational obligations where reasonably required.

What rights do you have regarding your data?

  • You may request information about whether your personal data are stored and how they are being used.
  • You may request correction of inaccurate data, restriction of processing where appropriate, or deletion where legally permissible.
  • You may object to certain uses of your personal information, especially where direct marketing or unnecessary processing is involved.
  • You may raise a complaint with the competent regulatory authority if you believe your data protection rights have been infringed.
POPIA & Data Protection

General information and mandatory information.

The operators of this website take the protection of personal information seriously. Personal data should be treated confidentially and in accordance with applicable legal and professional obligations, including relevant South African data protection principles such as POPIA where applicable.

Please note that data transmission over the internet, including communication by email, may be subject to security risks. Complete protection of data against access by third parties cannot be guaranteed.

Revocation of consent

Where data processing depends on your consent, you may revoke that consent at any time with future effect. An informal email request is generally sufficient. Data processed before we receive your request may still have been processed lawfully.

Right to complain to a regulatory authority

If there has been a breach of applicable data protection law, an affected person may raise the matter with a competent regulatory authority, including the South African Information Regulator where applicable.

Right to access, correction, blocking, restriction, or deletion

As permitted by law, you may request information about your stored personal data, including the origin, recipients, and purpose of processing. You may also request correction, restriction, blocking, or deletion where legally appropriate.

Right to data portability

Where technically feasible and legally applicable, you may request that data processed on the basis of consent or a contractual relationship be provided to you, or to another responsible party, in a standard machine-readable format.

Contact details published on this website are not to be used for unsolicited advertising or promotional messages. Mawonga Training reserves the right to take appropriate action against spam or unauthorised promotional contact.

Server log files and technical records

The website hosting environment may automatically collect and store information transmitted by your browser in server log files. These records may include:

  • Browser type and browser version.
  • Operating system used.
  • Referrer URL.
  • Hostname of the accessing computer.

  • IP address.

These technical records are used to help maintain secure and stable website operation and are not intentionally combined with other data sources unless necessary for lawful technical or security reasons.

This website may rely on standard browser technologies, cached content, or cookies used by essential functions or embedded third-party services.
