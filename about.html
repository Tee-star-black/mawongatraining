01Our purpose

When the people delivering care grow,
healthcare grows with them.

Mawonga Training exists to support the professionals behind patient care. We bring clinical education, professional ethics and business mentorship together to strengthen primary healthcare services.

01 / CARE

Support the carers.

Training that recognises the pressures of frontline work and keeps practitioner wellbeing visible.

02 / EMPOWER

Build capability.

Clear, practical knowledge that supports confident decisions and professional growth.

03 / STRENGTHEN

Improve the systems.

Better workflows, stronger teams and more responsive services for the communities you serve.

How we teach

Led from practice.
Built for application.

Sessions are facilitated by practising primary healthcare providers from Dr Mukudu & Partners. Teaching combines technical knowledge, mentorship and practical implementation guidance.

  • Clinical capability and sustainable practice
  • Clear, action-focused teaching
  • Support for teams, leadership and patient outreach
