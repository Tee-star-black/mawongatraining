Who We Are

Who leads the work.

Mawonga Training is a training and mentorship team led by Dr Mukudu and Partners. Sessions are delivered by practising primary healthcare providers with more than ten years of experience.

Mawonga Training exists to care for carers, empower people, and strengthen health systems. We build practical skills for clinical work in primary care and the business side of running a practice through clear, action-focused teaching.

Care for carers

We support the professionals who carry patient care every day, with training and mentorship that recognise the demands of frontline healthcare work.

Empower people

We focus on practical knowledge, confident decision-making, and real systems that help healthcare workers grow their capacity and serve more effectively.

Strengthen systems

We prepare practitioners to build better workflows, stronger teams, and more resilient primary care services in the communities they serve.

What We Do

Core training areas.

We provide clinical training for primary healthcare and entrepreneurial mentoring for practice owners. The work is designed to be usable in the real conditions of a busy healthcare setting.

Clinical skills

We strengthen practical clinical skills for frontline healthcare settings, with a clear focus on competent, safe, and responsive service delivery.

Medical entrepreneurship

We help healthcare professionals understand the business side of practice, including medical aids, coding, claims, pricing, and workable day-to-day systems.

SARS and finance basics

We provide guidance on SARS basics, simple finance structures, and practical business routines for nurse-led and primary care practices.

Biomedical ethics

Training includes professional ethics, defensible documentation, and readiness for the legal and professional standards that shape healthcare practice.

Leadership and data

We support stronger teams, clearer supervision, better quality routines, and more confident use of data to improve performance and decision-making.

Patient outreach

We help practices communicate better with patients and communities through outreach approaches that are practical, professional, and easier to sustain.

How We Train

How learning happens.

Mawonga Training is designed for healthcare workers who need useful guidance, not abstract theory. The learning model stays close to the realities of primary care practice and professional growth.

What participants can expect

  • Sessions are led by practising primary healthcare providers with real clinical and operational experience.
  • Training combines technical knowledge, mentorship, and implementation guidance.
  • The teaching style is simple, clear, and action-focused so participants can apply what they learn quickly.
  • Clinical capability and practice sustainability are addressed together rather than as separate problems.
